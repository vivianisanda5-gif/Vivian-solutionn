Drug Misuse Prevention

Prevention includes education, supportive relationships, healthy activities, and access to trustworthy guidance.

  • Learn the facts from reliable health sources.
  • Practice ways to respond to peer pressure.
  • Seek help from a trusted adult or professional.
  • Take part in sports, arts, clubs, and skills training.
  • Avoid taking medicines other than as directed by a qualified professional.

Educational Resources

General information to help individuals and communities understand substance-related concerns.

Possible Effects

Substance misuse can affect physical health, emotions, sleep, relationships, learning, and daily responsibilities. Effects vary depending on the substance and individual.

Treatment and Recovery

Support may include professional assessment, counseling, medical treatment, rehabilitation, and continuing care. Recovery is possible, and each person's needs differ.

Supporting Someone

Listen respectfully, avoid insults or blame, encourage professional assistance, and seek emergency help if the person is in immediate danger.

Get Help and Support

If you or someone you know is struggling with substance use, contact a qualified healthcare professional, counselor, hospital, or licensed treatment service.

Emergency warning: If someone is unconscious, having a seizure, struggling to breathe, or cannot be awakened after taking a substance, contact local emergency medical services immediately.

Seeking help is a positive step. People deserve care, privacy, and respect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does substance misuse only affect one type of person?

No. Substance-related problems can affect people from many backgrounds. Everyone should be treated with dignity and respect.

Can I diagnose someone from their behaviour?

No. Behaviour changes can have many causes. A qualified health professional can help assess concerns.

Does this website provide medical treatment?

No. This website provides general education and does not replace diagnosis or treatment by qualified professionals.
